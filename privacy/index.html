Cookie & Privacy Policy (UK)

Last updated October 19, 2026. This policy applies to visitors to veldanalytics.com in the United Kingdom.

1. Introduction

Our website, https://veldanalytics.com (the "website"), uses cookies and other related technologies (for convenience all of them are referred to as "cookies"). Some are placed by third parties whose services we use. This page explains what is used on our website, why, and how you can control it.

2. What are cookies?

A cookie is a small file that is sent along with the pages of a website and stored by your browser on your computer or another device. The information stored in it may be returned to our servers, or to the servers of the relevant third party, during a later visit.

3. What are scripts?

A script is a piece of program code that makes a website work properly and interactively. This code runs on your device or on a server.

4. What is a web beacon?

A web beacon (or pixel tag) is a small, invisible piece of text or image on a website that is used to monitor traffic. We do not use web beacons or advertising pixels on this website.

5. Types of cookies

5.1 Technical or functional cookies

These make sure parts of the website work properly and remember your preferences, for example your cookie choice. We may use these without your consent. On this website, your cookie choice is stored in your browser's local storage rather than in a cookie.

5.2 Statistics cookies

We use statistics cookies to understand how the website is used so we can improve it. We ask your permission before placing them. They are only set if you click Accept.

5.3 Marketing / tracking cookies

These are used to build profiles to show advertising or to track you across websites. We do not use any marketing or tracking cookies, and we do not run advertising on this website.

5.4 Social media

This website does not embed social media buttons or feeds. Links to our LinkedIn and portfolio pages are ordinary links; the other site's own policies apply once you click through.

If you click "Decline", they are not used. You can also disable cookies in your browser, but parts of the website may then not work properly.

8. Enabling, disabling and deleting cookies

You can use your internet browser to delete cookies automatically or manually, to block certain cookies, or to be notified each time a cookie is placed. For details, see the Help section of your browser. If you delete cookies, they will only be placed again if you consent when you next visit. You can also reopen the banner at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer.

9. Your rights with respect to personal data

You have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • The right to know why your personal data is needed, what will happen to it, and how long it will be kept.
  • Right of access: you can ask for the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: you can ask us to correct, complete, block or delete your personal data.
  • If you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time and ask for your personal data to be deleted.
  • Right to data portability: you can ask for your personal data to be provided so you can transfer it to another organisation.
  • Right to object: you can object to our processing of your data, and we will comply unless there are justified grounds for the processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below. If you have a complaint about how we handle your data, we would like to hear from you first, but you also have the right to complain to the supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
