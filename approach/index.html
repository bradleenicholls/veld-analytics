How we work

The Veld Method

A structured route from reporting confusion to trusted decision-making.

01 · Map the landscape

Understand where complexity sits

We start by understanding your business questions, reporting pain points, current Google Analytics 4 setup, Google Ads and social ad accounts, and stakeholders. This creates a clear view of where the complexity actually sits.

02 · Define the signal

Remove ambiguity before building

We clarify the KPIs, definitions, owners, audiences, and decisions your reporting needs to support. This removes ambiguity before anything gets built.

03 · Build the structure

Turn messy inputs into usable outputs

We create the data models, dashboard logic, reporting assets, and documentation needed to support trusted analytics. This is where messy inputs become usable outputs.

04 · Validate the numbers

Trust is built through QA, not hope

We test the logic, reconcile outputs, check assumptions, and identify gaps before dashboards are treated as trusted. Trust is built through QA, not hope.

05 · Guide the decisions

Better dashboards, better decisions

We help teams understand the outputs, use the reporting, and improve the system over time. The goal is not just better dashboards. It is better decisions.
