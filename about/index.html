About

Veld Analytics exists to bring direction to Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, SEO, and social reporting.

Businesses do not need more noise from their data. They need direction.

Many businesses already have Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, SEO, and social accounts running. What they often lack is trust: trust in the tracking, trust in the numbers, trust in the definitions, and trust that the dashboard is telling the right story.

The name "Veld" draws from the idea of open landscape and clear perspective. It reflects how we work: mapping the terrain, finding the signal, and creating a clear route through complexity.

We help businesses diagnose Google Analytics 4 and ad tracking problems, define the right KPIs, and build trusted Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, SEO, and social dashboards their teams can rely on.

Our focus is simple: make Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, SEO, and social reporting clearer, more useful, and easier to trust.

Background

Over half a decade in applied analytics, across industries.

Veld Analytics is led by a South African-born consultant, the origin of the name and a fitting reminder that clarity should feel like open ground, not a maze to navigate. That background is paired with an MBA and experience on both sides of analytics delivery: inside agencies serving multiple clients at pace, and in-house, embedded within a single leadership team making high-stakes calls. Both perspectives shape how every engagement is run today.

That experience spans finance, e-commerce, and retail: industries where leadership needs the number on the slide to hold up under real scrutiny. Along the way, helping people understand, interpret, and trust their own data became more than a job description. It's the conviction behind Veld Analytics, and the standard every engagement is held to.
